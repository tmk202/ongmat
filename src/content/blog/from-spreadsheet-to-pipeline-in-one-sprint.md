You do not need a platform team to remove the worst manual step.

Most manual work survives because nobody has priced it. Count the hours, count the corrections, and the business case usually writes itself.

Automate the step with a clear input and a clear output first. Leave the judgement calls for later, and make every automated change reversible.

An approval gate only works if the reviewer can see the change in one screen. Otherwise the gate becomes a rubber stamp with extra steps.
