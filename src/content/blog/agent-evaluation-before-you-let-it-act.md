Test the tool calls, not just the prose. Most failures are wrong actions, not wrong words.

An agent is a permissions problem wearing a language model. Decide what it may read, what it may write, and what it must never touch.

Keep the task narrow. Classification, extraction and routing are reliable. Open-ended decision making under ambiguity is not, and should stay with a person.

Test the actions, not the prose. A fluent answer with the wrong tool call is a worse outcome than a clumsy answer with the right one.
